**Explanation of our ER Diagram:**

**Notification Types:**  
**For Buyer:**

* Outbid by another bidder  
* Followed seller has a new item on auction  
* New auction based on preferences  
* Auction start/end reminder  
* Outcome of auction

**For Seller:**

* New bid on item on auction  
* New follower  
* Item is on watchlist  
* Outcome of auction  
* Complaint registered by system  
* Feedback registered by system

Users have the ability to state their preference on what kind of notifications they would like to get (e.g. users may choose to not receive notifications when their favourite seller posts a new auction or changes in status of the items on their watchlist).

**Outcome Types:**

- Item sold to highest bidder  
- Item sold to “buy now” bidder  
- Unsold  
- Cancelled auction  
- Admin shut down (this is dependent on whether we can have an admin role)

**Filters for Items:**

- Categories  
- Price Range  
- Search Query   
- Condition (second hand/used, like new, new, heavily used, etc.)  
- Whether listings have images?  
- Status of Auction (Ending soon, Brand New, Closed, Active, etc.)
